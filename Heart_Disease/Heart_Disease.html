Task definition and four-input decision¶

Use only the processed Cleveland part of the UCI Heart Disease dataset. The recorded target num=0 means absence; num=1,2,3,4 is mapped to presence. num is never an input.

Four attributes are selected before evaluating the model: age, cp, thalach, and oldpeak. They cover age, symptom category, exercise heart rate and exercise-related ST depression. This is an interpretable classroom choice, not a claim of optimal features. No fifth attribute is used at training or prediction time.

The teaching steps and SVC are retained. Dataset-specific adaptations are ? handling, a binary target, a stratified 80/20 split with seed 4, and training-only imputation/scaling. Only categorical cp is label encoded; numerical measurements retain their meaning.

Dataset citation: Janosi, A., Steinbrunn, W., Pfisterer, M., & Detrano, R. (1989). Heart Disease. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C52P4X. License: CC BY 4.0. This experiment is not a medical diagnostic tool.

Step 5: Label encoding¶

5.1 Fit encoders; 5.2 encode output; 5.3 encode inputs¶

The category vocabularies are fixed by the dataset definitions, not learned from the held-out labels. LabelEncoder sorts labels. For Titanic this means Sibling codes are One=0, Three=1, Two=2, Zero=3; they are not sibling counts. For Heart Disease cp codes 1-4 map to 0-3, and num > 0 supplies the binary target. Numeric measurements are not converted into arbitrary category labels. These encoders will be saved for inference.

In [8]:
category_schema = {'cp': [1, 2, 3, 4]}

Learning reflection¶

The important connection is that deployment must apply exactly the representation used during training. Saving only an estimator would leave the app dependent on manually recreating its encoders and feature order. Saving them together and verifying reload predictions makes the training-to-application path reproducible. The four ML phases are demonstrated without mistaking a small held-out score for broad real-world validity.
